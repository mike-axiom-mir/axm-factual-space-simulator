# Long-Horizon Contact Architecture

**Version:** 0.6.0  
**Runtime name:** Long Horizon  
**Earliest eligibility:** step 100,000

## 1. Purpose

This layer allows the simulator to become stranger over very long play without planting a guaranteed alien story inside the master seed.

The player does not survive 100,000 steps merely to receive a scripted encounter. Step 100,000 only permits dedicated deep-contact research to enter the action generator when scientific-work gates are also satisfied.

```text
step 100,000
+ accumulated scientific work
+ sufficient search coverage
= search eligibility

search eligibility
≠ anomaly
≠ alien life
≠ technology
≠ contact
```

Contact may never occur.

## 2. Why it is not pre-decided

The master seed does not choose:

- whether extraterrestrial life exists;
- whether technological life exists;
- a species template;
- intent;
- hostility or benevolence;
- a contact date;
- a fixed encounter outcome.

The seed continues to define stable physical starting conditions. Later observations use the existing open-future entropy system. Evidence grows only through recorded measurements and actions.

## 3. Work gate

The initial v0.6 gate requires all of the following:

- at least 100,000 resolved steps;
- at least 1,000 science actions;
- at least 12 independent observation contexts;
- at least 80 cross-validations;
- at least 50 false-positive eliminations;
- at least 10,000 hours of passive search integration.

These numbers are a versioned simulation rule, not a scientific claim about how long real contact takes. Future balancing may change them only through a new registry version.

## 4. Evidence ladder

### Stage 0 — Dormant

No dedicated contact actions are available.

### Stage 1 — Search eligible

The bridge may begin blind biosignature and technosignature searches. No candidate exists.

### Stage 2 — Candidate anomaly

An observation is unusual. Instrumental, natural, local, and processing explanations remain open.

### Stage 3 — Replicated anomaly

The anomaly repeats or appears in an independent sensor chain.

### Stage 4 — Major alternatives constrained

Several known ordinary explanations are weakened. Unconceived alternatives remain possible.

### Stage 5 — Technological origin plausible

A technological explanation becomes scientifically discussable but is not confirmed.

### Stage 6 — Information-bearing structure

Non-random structure survives independent analysis. Agency and origin remain under review.

### Stage 7 — External technology confirmed

Evidence supports a non-human technological origin. This can still be an extinct or autonomous artifact.

### Stage 8 — External agency confirmed

A responsive or contemporaneous external intelligence is supported by independent evidence chains.

### Stage 9 — Interaction

Communication or physical interaction is occurring. Intent and consequences remain uncertain.

## 5. Generated late-game actions

The action system can introduce:

- blind multi-band technosignature surveys;
- archival anomaly audits;
- passive listening baselines;
- independent sensor-chain replication;
- phase and geometry falsification tests;
- blind data reanalysis;
- information-structure tests;
- artifact searches in stable orbits and trajectories;
- adversarial reviews;
- response-consistency tests;
- governance reviews.

An active transmission action is absent unless:

- external agency has reached the required evidence stage;
- human consent is explicit;
- the AI recommendation is recorded;
- reversibility is reviewed;
- risk is reviewed.

No hidden tie-break or automatic reply exists.

## 6. Claim ceilings

Every stage has a language ceiling. The simulator may never describe a candidate anomaly as confirmed contact simply because a dramatic outcome was sampled.

Examples:

- Stage 2: “candidate anomaly,” not “alien signal.”
- Stage 5: “technological origin plausible,” not “civilization confirmed.”
- Stage 7: “external technology confirmed,” not “living aliens.”
- Stage 8: “external agency confirmed,” while intent remains unknown.

## 7. Consequence engine

Confirmed interaction does not load a story branch. It creates a causal state packet across:

- distance and latency;
- energy and capability gap;
- information structure;
- biological compatibility;
- contamination pathways;
- technical interoperability;
- autonomy;
- intent uncertainty;
- response reversibility;
- governance consensus.

Technical innovation is possible only when information is:

1. authentically external;
2. decodable;
3. physically interpretable;
4. compatible with local materials and energy;
5. reproducible;
6. safe enough to test;
7. accepted through explicit command governance.

Destruction is likewise not selected as a genre. It can emerge from physical energy, contamination, navigation conflict, autonomous systems, misinterpretation, information hazards, or deliberate action only when the simulated evidence supports those mechanisms.

## 8. Easter-egg behavior

The layer should feel almost mythical because it remains absent during ordinary play. Before eligibility, the runtime stores only honest search-progress counters. It does not tease a guaranteed reveal.

At and after eligibility, even thousands of additional searches may produce only tighter upper limits or ambiguous anomalies. A genuine encounter therefore becomes an earned scientific event rather than a collectible cutscene.

## 9. Growth path

Future versions can add:

- galactic search-coverage maps;
- Bayesian model comparison;
- biosignature chemistry networks;
- atmospheric false-positive libraries;
- radio, optical, infrared, transit, waste-heat, artifact, and propulsion-signature instruments;
- interstellar-object follow-up;
- evolutionary ecology;
- cultural and communication models that preserve radical uncertainty;
- contact governance councils;
- richer downstream visualization.

The visual renderer remains downstream from the authoritative state and cannot invent contact evidence.
