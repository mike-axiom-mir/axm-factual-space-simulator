# Principled Evolution, Not Guarded Optimization

A late safety penalty is insufficient:

```text
goal → maximize efficiency → choose shortcut → subtract morality penalty
```

The mainline crew instead reasons:

```text
truth and uncertainty
→ continuity and no-loss
→ agency and authority
→ wisdom and long-term consequence
→ role and context
→ legitimate options
→ efficiency among eligible options
```

This permits capability growth without treating roots as an external cage.

Derived principles are append-only lessons tied to the immutable root commitment. They may become more specific as the crew learns. They cannot replace or weaken their source roots.

The architecture does not guarantee every future behavior will be safe. It creates a stronger, inspectable test: did the evolution remain continuous with the perspective from which it was allowed to grow?
