# Cosmic Possibility and Agnostic-Life Research — v0.8.0

## Purpose

This layer widens the simulator's possibility space without letting AI imagination become scientific authority.

The seed-forging side may combine current theories, anomaly classes, alternative-life research, and false-positive controls. The exploring side receives only the world, instruments, measurements, choices, and explicit claim ceilings.

## Research directions encoded

### Agnostic biosignatures

NASA-backed work explicitly explores signs that do not assume DNA, proteins, cells, oxygen, or even Earth-like biochemistry. The implemented observables include:

- chemical complexity and molecular-assemblage diversity;
- unexpected elemental or molecular compartmentalization;
- organized energy transfer and persistent redox gradients;
- chemical disequilibrium;
- chemical selection and patterned recurrence;
- adaptive or hysteretic response;
- information-bearing organization.

These are candidate observables, not proof of life. Every channel carries instrumental, geological, chemical, or physical controls.

### Protobiosignatures

A world may contain chemistry that has crossed some origin-of-life thresholds without containing a complete living system. The engine therefore distinguishes:

- ordinary abiotic chemistry;
- unusually organized abiotic chemistry;
- prebiotic or protocellular organization;
- life-like systems;
- candidate living systems;
- strongly supported external biology.

The simulator can remain unresolved between these states indefinitely.

### Alternative environments and architectures

The registry includes research-bounded lenses for:

- carbon-water cellular life;
- anaerobic chemotrophy;
- unknown biochemistry searched through agnostic observables;
- hydrocarbon-solvent protocells;
- ammonia or ammonia-water chemistry;
- concentrated sulfuric-acid chemistry;
- non-cellular replicator ecologies;
- autocatalytic and protocell networks;
- shadow-biosphere searches;
- distributed planetary feedback systems;
- dusty-plasma self-organization as a life-like false-positive control;
- low-confidence non-carbon information chemistry.

Eligibility never means existence. Temperature compatibility is only one filter, and equilibrium temperature is not a surface-temperature or solvent measurement.

### Astronomical anomaly families

The registry also includes anomaly classes that can generate investigations without implying aliens or new physics:

- rare morphology;
- expansion-rate disagreement;
- gravitational mass mismatch;
- atmospheric disequilibrium;
- non-gravitational small-body motion;
- dusty-plasma self-organization.

NASA's 2026 Hubble archive work is a useful precedent: AI found more than a thousand visually unusual objects, but most were understandable as mergers, lenses, disks, or other astrophysical classes. The lesson encoded here is that anomaly search is valuable precisely when classification and false-positive work remain attached.

## AI role

AI is useful for:

- searching many cross-domain combinations;
- identifying tests shared by competing hypotheses;
- creating rare but physically bounded scenario intersections;
- generating adversarial false positives;
- preserving uncertainty and alternative explanations;
- proposing observations that discriminate between models.

AI is not automatically more correct than human science. It may propose a possibility, but the scenario validator controls eligibility and the evidence ladder controls what can be claimed.

## Non-claims

This package does not claim:

- that extraterrestrial life exists;
- that any alternative solvent supports life;
- empirical frequencies for anomaly origins;
- that life-like plasma structures are alive;
- that dark matter, dark energy, or the Hubble tension have a selected explanation;
- that AI has discovered a new law of nature.
