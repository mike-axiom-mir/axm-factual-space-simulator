# Action Report — v0.8.0

## Requested direction

Create scientifically broader universe/anomaly/life possibilities, but have one AI reason and seal the seed before a different AI or human explores it without knowing what is coming.

- Six anomaly families and twelve life-architecture lenses.

- Blind Forge private packet.
- Public player-only mission bundle.
- Pre-play cryptographic commitment.
- Public/private leak scanner.
- Ten agnostic investigation actions.
- Physics-like noisy measurement resolution with explicit detection thresholds.
- Evidence ladder with convergent-test requirements.
- Hash-chained public event ledger.
- Sealed resolution receipts.

- Separate player-only ZIP for handing to another AI.

## Important design decisions

### AI reasoning is a proposal system

AI synthesis explores combinations and creates the pre-play possibility packet. It does not become empirical authority. Registry validation and evidence ceilings remain above the proposal.

### The hidden seed is not the whole future

The private packet fixes a hidden starting phenomenon. Choices, instruments, geometry, runtime entropy, thresholds, and resource consequences remain open.

### Possibility priors are labelled

The bundled origin-class weights are entertainment design priors. They are not presented as real frequencies for extraterrestrial biology, novel physics, or false positives.

### Player blindness is physical, not theatrical

The exploring AI should receive only the `player_public` directory. The private packet is not embedded in the HTML, JSON, source facts, or action descriptions.

## Repair made during testing

The first leak scanner treated the phrase `candidate_living_system` as leaked whenever the evidence ladder publicly listed that possible category. That was too strict: the player may know all possible categories, but not which one was selected. The scanner was repaired to protect the selected private IDs, packet keys, and resolution secret rather than banning public taxonomy.
