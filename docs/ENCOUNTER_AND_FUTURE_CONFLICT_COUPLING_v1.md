# Encounter and Future Conflict Coupling v1

## Effects-first architecture

Encounters affect physical and operational channels:

- kinetic impact;
- pressure loss;
- thermal load;
- radiation;
- electromagnetic interference;
- sensor ambiguity;
- communication loss;
- generation loss;
- navigation uncertainty;
- data integrity;
- medical events;
- docking contact loads.

The same channels can later support realistic conflict, rescue, debris, unknown-object, environmental, and accident scenarios.

## Intent boundary

Damage does not prove hostility.

The simulator stores observations, measured effects, declared intent when available, uncertainty, and command decisions. Unknown, natural, accidental, cooperative, and hostile explanations remain separate until evidence supports an update.

## Space conflict boundary

Version 1 models geometry, detection, maneuver, power, thermal consequences, communications, sensors, pressure, structural damage, crew effects, repair, and survival.

It does not contain real-world weapon-construction instructions. It also does not use magic shields or damage numbers disconnected from the ship.
