# AXM Factual Star Adventure Simulator Roadmap

## Completed through v0.6

- deterministic factual star-system generation;
- truth, formula, source, technology, and entropy registries;
- open-future entropy and replay receipts;
- four explicit command-authority modes;
- physics-coupled measurements and trajectories;
- persistent causal-thread action growth;
- fail-closed public-randomness boundary;
- source-pinned human ship-technology cores;
- source-neutral exploration-function mapping;
- requirement-versus-capability enforcement;
- long-horizon contact registry with step 100,000 and scientific-work gates;
- staged evidence and claim ceilings;
- no hidden alien existence, intent, morality, or encounter schedule;
- passive-first search, independent replication, false-positive elimination, and governance gates;
- consequence generation through physical, epistemic, biological, technical, and governance axes.

## v0.7 candidate — evidence instruments and false-positive libraries

- instrument-specific technosignature channels;
- stellar activity, plasma, radio-frequency interference, detector persistence, compression artifacts, and pipeline-bias libraries;
- biosignature context models for atmosphere, geology, disequilibrium, preservation, and contamination;
- Bayesian or likelihood-ratio evidence updates with clearly exposed priors;
- blind-analysis and independent-observer packets;
- search-volume accounting so non-detections constrain only what was actually searched.

## v0.8 candidate — information and artifact physics

- signal propagation, dispersion, Doppler drift, scintillation, bandwidth, coding, and error correction;
- delayed beacons whose creators may be extinct;
- artifact age, orbit stability, degradation, autonomy, and provenance uncertainty;
- technical-knowledge compatibility checks;
- innovation gates requiring reproducibility, material availability, energy feasibility, safety, and local understanding;
- information-hazard and irreversible-disclosure models.

## v0.9 candidate — biology and contamination

- alternative biochemistry envelopes without pretending every possibility is equally likely;
- ecosystem energetics and evolutionary contingency;
- forward and backward contamination;
- quarantine, sample handling, and planetary-protection governance;
- microbial, complex, extinct, and non-technological life as distinct evidence paths.

## v1.x candidate — interstellar-scale history

- versioned galactic population ensembles using conditional Drake-style, timing, detectability, and expansion assumptions;
- explicit observer-selection sensitivity;
- light-cone and communication-latency geometry;
- multi-generation expeditions and institutional memory;
- encounters that may remain permanently unresolved;
- no canon outcome across seeds or campaigns.

## Technology-specific organs

- source-reviewed life support and consumables;
- electric-propulsion performance maps where published;
- cryogenic storage, boil-off, transfer, and plumbing;
- rendezvous, docking, plume, and proximity operations;
- mission-duration and crew-exposure limits;
- modular launch and assembly architectures.

## Later visualization branch

An immersive renderer may consume the same system, event, sensor, evidence, and causality packets for richer bridge scenes. Rendering remains downstream and non-authoritative. Visual spectacle may never raise the evidence stage or rewrite physics.
