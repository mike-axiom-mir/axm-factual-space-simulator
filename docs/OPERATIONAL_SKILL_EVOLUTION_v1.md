# Operational Skill Evolution v1

The simulator contains 22 role competencies. It computes operational learning pressure from non-nominal metrics, related faults, role responsibility, control use and qualification gaps. This is not a personality or desire prediction.

Exposure does not grant skill. Promotion requires evidence such as knowledge checks, part-task simulation, integrated off-nominal simulation, supervised operation, independent operation and verifier signoff. Evidence and promotion ledgers are hash-chained.
