Persistent expedition atlas

Real catalog anchors, simulated frontier locations, and crew observations share one map without sharing one truth label. Re-rendering an old expedition can improve the visual experience, but it cannot change the preserved location, visit, or event hashes.

Map is not engine capability: a coordinate can be visible and selectable while physical travel remains unavailable, delayed, or impossible for the current ship.
